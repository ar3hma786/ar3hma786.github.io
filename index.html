Boltberry Circuit

Boltberry Circuit is a YouTube channel of original animated short stories about Bolt, a curious robot with a big heart, his tiny friend Pixel, and Dr. Amara Okafor, the engineer who built him.

Boltberry Studio is the private production tool behind the channel. It is used only by the owner of Boltberry Circuit to produce the channel's own original animated videos, upload them to the channel through the official YouTube Data API, and read the channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API. It has no other users.
